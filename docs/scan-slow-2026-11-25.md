# Channel scan "extremely slow" on 1.0.2 (2026-11-25)

Owner's report from the radio, firmware 1.0.2 (`a4b7bdb` "Version 1.0.2"): "channel scan is extremely, extremely slow,
something is wrong". Before it (2026-11-24, on 1.0.1): "a zone I made scans very slowly, classic and fast; All
channels is fast". Branch `scanslow` (from `ddm25` e9b3c65). **Host-tested only; nothing here was measured on the
radio.**

## 1. Is it a regression from 1.0.1? No, not by the code

What changed in the firmware between `v1.0.1` and `a4b7bdb` (`git diff --stat v1.0.1..a4b7bdb -- tools integration
src`): the zone scan change (`zonescan`: `scanfast/`, `scanx/radio_scanprio.inc`, `tools/plus_fastscan.py`), the VFO
step key (UI only), the RAM saving (`plus_ramsave.py`: frame buffer of the safe area, one slot buffer each in
settings / storage). Nothing else reaches the scan.

| Suspect | What the code says |
|---|---|
| zone scan visits more channels / all zones | no: `scan_next_pos` walks `sm->zone` (read once when the zone is chosen); 1.0.2 only stopped reading the start channel at every stop |
| per-step codeplug flash reads | fewer in 1.0.2: one `codeplug_channel` a stop instead of three |
| longer dwell, timer, debounce | no: `SCAN_SETTLE_MS` 50, dwell from the settings, poll 25 ms / tick 10 ms unchanged; 1.0.2 only added the 20 ms retune guard (squelch opening) and `FS_SETTLE_RETUNE_MS` 25 (the fast rule's first reading) counted from the end of the retune |
| fast / classic default flipped | no: `FS_DEFAULT_FAST` 1, the switch in RAM (`fs_set_fast`), unchanged |
| a redraw or a log write per step | no: the UI task runs below the radio task; no search / hit log write in the scan |
| All channels zone included | no zone-specific path; All channels is just another `zone_t` |
| frame buffer change (`ramsave`) | display only (UI task, own bus); not on the radio task |

The same host model (`tests/plus/test_zonescan_radio.inc`, real radio state machine on the fake board) gives the
same figures on 1.0.1, 1.0.2 and `ddm25` head with the model's 15 ms noise dip: quiet FM stop 152.5 ms classic,
53.3 ms fast, in a made zone and in All channels (with no read time; at 3 ms a channel read 1.0.1's three reads a
stop made classic 290 ms, 1.0.2 is 152 ms).

**`ddm25` head (after 1.0.2) is not slower either.** The P25 RX group list (`rxgl`) reads 1 list + up to 32 contacts
only when the native receiver *takes* a P25 channel (`native_lab.c` `channel_service`), and in a zone scan
`scan_allows()` lets it take a channel only in a hold (`SCAN_PH_HOLD`): never on a quiet stop. 33 small flash reads
(< 1 ms) once per take. The soft P25 probe is the P25 *search* only and off by default. The rest (ISS hint, NAC on the
caller line, byline) is UI.

Codeplug read cost on the owner's codeplug (local backup 2026-11-21, aggregates only): the settings area holds 13
channel tags, 234 bytes, one page. A `codeplug_kv_get` there is about 80 SPI transactions (counted from the code) (CRC and two walks in
`chain_check`, the walk again in `raw_next`), roughly 1 ms at 21 MHz; an FM channel read pays one, a P25 channel
about three. Milliseconds, not seconds.

## 2. What does make a scan "extremely slow": a quiet channel held for the resume delay

On the model, the scan has a cliff that no version moved far enough. Right after a retune the FD6818's noise
reading reads low (like a carrier) for a moment (OpenDMR trusts it from 20 ms; nobody measured it on this radio; the
model uses 15 ms). If that dip outlasts the guards, the squelch opens at the stop's first poll, the classic rules see an
open squelch after their 50 ms settle and **hold the quiet channel; when the squelch closes the scan waits the resume
delay (2 s by default) before it moves on**. The fast rule's first readings meet the same dip and hand the stop to
the classic rules, so fast does the same. That is 2 s a channel instead of 53 / 152 ms: "extremely slow", in both
algorithms.

Host model, quiet zone Mine (12 FM channels, one band), ms a stop (tune to tune), 20 s runs; v1.0.1 from the same model
on a `v1.0.1` tree (3 channel reads a stop), 1.0.2 = `ddm25` head (1 read):

| Dip after a retune | read / channel | v1.0.1 classic / fast | 1.0.2 classic / fast | `scanslow` classic / fast |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15 ms (the model so far) | 0 ms | 152.5 / 53.3 | 152.5 / 52.6 | 152.5 / 52.6 |
| 15 ms | 3 ms | 290 / 53.6 (quiet channels held) | 151.8 / 53.4 | 151.8 / 53.4 |
| 20 ms | 3 ms | **2009 / 2009** | **264** / 53.1 | (not run; 25 covers it) |
| 25 ms | 0 ms | **2000** / 114 | **2000 / 643** | 152.5 / 52.6 |
| 25 ms | 3 ms | **2009 / 2009** | **1803 / 624** | 151.8 / 53.4 |
| 35 ms | 0 ms | **2000 / 2000** | **2000 / 2000** | 152.5 / 81.8 |
| 35 ms | 3 ms | **2009 / 2009** | **2003 / 2003** | 151.8 / 93.0 |
| 45 ms | 0 ms | **2000 / 2000** | **2000 / 2000** | 152.5 / 83.7 |
| 35 ms, scan dwell 75 ms | 0 ms | | **2000** (classic) | 122 (classic; short holds, no 2 s wait) |

Bold: every quiet channel held for the 2 s resume delay. 1.0.2 moved the cliff a little (it tolerates a 20 ms dip with
slow reads in fast), it did not make it worse. With a dip of 15 ms in one band and 40 ms on a VHF / UHF jump (All
channels: channels 1-8 UHF, 9-16 VHF, two jumps a pass; Mine jumps at 8 of its 12 stops):

| | 1.0.2 All channels | 1.0.2 Mine | `scanslow` All channels | `scanslow` Mine |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| classic, ms a stop | 383 | **1385** | 152.5 | 152.5 |
| fast, ms a stop | 225 | **1200** | 56.8 | 72.9 |

The made zone vs All channels pattern of the 1.0.1 report comes out of the same mechanism if the dip is longer after a
VHF / UHF jump than after a step within one band (likely for a synthesizer, unmeasured): All channels (by channel
number) seldom jumps band, a made zone that mixes bands jumps at most stops.

So the cause is probably **not a regression but a fragile timing assumption** that 1.0.1's fast scan (2.8x, its stops
start reading 30 ms after the retune) and 1.0.2's guards (20 / 25 ms) both rest on. It is **not proven**: the radio's
dip has never been measured. 1.0.2's `scanst` already tells it apart (see 4).

## 3. The fix (branch `scanslow`, `FS_DIP_GUARD`, both algorithms)

`integration/radiofoundry/scanfast/` (`radio_scanfast.inc`, `scanfast.h/.c`, `scanfast_view.c`) and one hook in
`tools/plus_fastscan.py`. Not touched: `native_lab.c`, `p25_lab.c`, `voice.c`, xband. No new limits; the fast /
classic switch (held 0) works as before. Zone and VFO scans only (dual watch and the ring keep their own timing).

1. **Squelch guard up to 50 ms.** After a scan retune the squelch doesn't open until the retune is `FS_DIP_MS` (50 ms =
   `SCAN_SETTLE_MS`) old, cut to the dwell less two polls (dwell 150: 50 ms; dwell 75: 25 ms) and never shorter than
   1.0.2's 20 / 25 ms. The classic rules ignore readings younger than 50 ms anyway; a real signal now opens the
   squelch at the first poll after 50 ms and holds the scan one poll (25 ms) later than before. No noise burst on the
   speaker at each channel either.
2. **Fast rule tolerates the dip.** A reading that is not quiet while the retune is younger than 50 ms no longer hands
   the stop to the classic rules: the quiet span starts again and then counts only readings from 50 ms on, over 30 ms
   (four readings) instead of 20. A not-quiet reading after 50 ms hands over as before. AM / broadcast: as before.
   With a short dip nothing changes (the first reading at 30 ms is already quiet).
3. **No resume delay after a false hold.** A hold whose squelch opened while the retune was younger than 50 ms (a
   short dwell's guard) and that heard no carrier after the settle (`scan_t.heard`) moves on as soon as the squelch
   closes instead of waiting 2 s. A real station hears its carrier and waits as before.
4. **Counters for the radio.** `scanst.cost` gains two fields: `DIPS` (stops whose readings were not quiet while the
   retune was under 50 ms old) and `DIP_HOLDS` (holds that ended by rule 3).

`FS_DIP_GUARD 0` builds 1.0.2's behaviour; the host test builds both and prints before / after.

**Costs, host model** (`tools/test_fastscan.py`): with the 15 ms dip every row of the fast scan model table is
unchanged in speed; the catch time of a station is about 30 ms later (the guard). The "FM around the squelch line"
row (a noise reading that crosses the line at random every ms) catches fewer overs: 16 channels classic 40 -> 37,
fast 46 -> 42 of 46, false quiet 2 -> 1; 32 channels fast 35 -> 33 of 45, false quiet 0 -> 2; 80 channels fast
21 -> 18 of 44. Every other row is within one over and one false quiet of before, except three DMR rows at a 30 ms
lag that move by two either way (28 / 47 more, 24 fewer). This is the price of the 50 ms guard for a station hovering
on the squelch line; if it shows on the radio, the guard can be made shorter for classic (the fix's rule 3 alone then
keeps quiet channels to about 125 ms a stop on the model).

Cost (`--plus --stack-check --verify-rebuild`, on `ddm25` e9b3c65): zero warnings, forced rebuild identical; FLASH
890,428 -> 890,868 B (+440, 98.86 %), static RAM 204,968 -> 204,992 B (+24; 6,464 B free, the 1,500 B floor kept). 35 ms dip, FM station 3 dB over the line in overs: 16 of 16 caught both
ways, no false quiet.

## 4. What needs the radio

- Run the scan that is slow (fast, then classic) on **1.0.2 first** and read `python pc/radiotools/plus_live.py --port
  COM14 --count 1 --scan-stats` after 30 s: if `scanst.st` NOTICED / HELD is close to STOPS with nothing on the air,
  this is the mechanism (quiet channels held). `scanst.slow` CAUSE `held` / `open` / `noise` and `scanst.fb` REASON
  `noise` or `open` point the same way; `scanst.cost` BANDS high says band jumps.
- Then the `scanslow` build: the same run. Expected: NOTICED / HELD near 0, PASS_MS / PASS_STOPS about 55-95 ms (fast)
  or 150 ms (classic), and `scanst.cost` DIPS says how often the radio's dip outlasts 30 ms (close to STOPS: the
  dip is longer than the old guards; near 0: the dip was not the cause, send the lines).
- A weak FM station near the squelch line, both algorithms (row SF-4): found as before.
- Not covered: a dip longer than 50 ms (the classic settle itself would be wrong), and resume "stop" with a short dwell
  (a false hold there still stops the scan, as in 1.0.2).

## 5. The radio's figures (verifier, 2026-11-25): the slow zone is a P25 zone and the strength reads high

Read on the owner's 1.0.2 with `plus_live --scan-stats` after about 60 s in the slow zone (read only). The owner: the
zone holds five P25 channels and **nothing was on the air nearby**.

| Line | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `scanst.st` | fast, 63,967 ms, 33 stops, noticed 33, held 33, fast quiet 0, fall-back 34 | every stop held |
| `scanst.slow` | 1,928 ms, P25, step 0 ms, `held` | a stop is 1.9 s; no slow step (the channel reads are not it) |
| `scanst.fb` | P25, noise 94 (line 60: quiet), RSSI -72 dBm, `strength` | the noise reading says nothing, the strength says a carrier |
| `scanst.cost` | gap 11, step 0, no band jump | no dip pattern, no slow step |
| native `0x21 [12]` | stops 34, carriers 34, takes 34, synced 0, quiet 33, cooled 0 | each stop taken, no frame, no cooldown hit |

So section 2's dip is **not** what slows this zone. The mechanism, from the code:

1. A P25 stop counts the strength as a carrier (DMR's line: -100 dBm at squelch 5; the fast rule 6 dB under it). The
   radio reads -72 dBm with nothing on the air: the FD6818's strength formula has no board correction on the DM-32
   (docs/plus.md, the search's floor; the DM1801 Pro applies a calibration table, the DM-32 none). Every P25 stop is a
   "carrier": the fast rule hands it to the classic rules, the classic rules probe and hold it.
2. The native receiver takes it, hears no frame for `SCAN_P25_PROBE_MS` (1.2 s) and gives it back with a cooldown.
3. The next stop is a carrier too, but the native receiver may not take it during `SCAN_P25_SETTLE_MS` (0.7 s) after the
   give back: the scan holds there, then the take, 1.2 s, give back... 0.7 + 1.2 s = the 1.9 s a stop measured.
4. The cooldown keeps **one** channel (`scan_skip_pos`): in a zone of several channels each give back overwrites the one
   before, so no channel is ever passed over (cooled 0) and the 8 s "again" cooldown never starts.

Not a regression: `native_lab.c`, the P25 strength rule (`plus_features.py`) and the fast rule's strength check are the
same in v1.0.1 and 1.0.2 (`git diff v1.0.1..a4b7bdb -- integration/radiofoundry/native tools/plus_scan.py`: empty). It
fits the 1.0.1 report too ("a zone I made scans very slowly, All channels fast"), if that zone was the P25 one.

**Fix (`fsc_floor`, radio_scanfast.inc, zone scan, both algorithms):** a P25 stop whose take heard no frame at all
(`native_lab_scan_quiet`) notes the strongest strength it was held on; a second no-frame take within 6 dB confirms it,
and from then on, for `FS_P25_FLOOR_MS` (120 s from the last take), a strength less than `FS_P25_FLOOR_DB` (10 dB) over
it is no carrier on that channel (the classic P25 rule doesn't probe or hold on it, the fast rule reads it quiet). Any
reading of the channel with no carrier by strength (past the settle) drops the note, and so does a take that hears
frames: a real carrier comes and goes, the radio's false strength stays. The first version without these two rules
failed the existing P25-in-a-zone-scan model (`test_zscan.inc`, 16 channels, 2 s calls, receiver deaf to C4FM: caught
20-43 % instead of >= 50 %): a take on a call's tail learned the call's own strength and hid the next calls. Eight
channels kept (oldest replaced), cleared at each scan start, the scanned zone only. The noise reading counts as before;
a signal 10 dB over the learned level is a carrier again. Cost with a steady false strength: two 1.9 s stops a channel
at the start of a scan and again every two minutes. New counters at the end of `scanst.cost`: P25_LEARNED
(confirmations), P25_MASKED.

Host model (`test_zonescan_p25_strength`, 60 s runs, built as 1.0.2 and as this branch): five P25 channels, the strength
reading 53 dB high (-72 dBm quiet), nothing on the air: 1.0.2 1,900 ms a stop (the radio's 1,928), now 73 ms fast / 204
ms classic on average including the two learning passes (about 20 s); a P25 signal of -95 dBm (read -42) in overs with
frames on one of them: noticed 10 of 18 on 1.0.2, 13 of 18 now (the overs during the two learning passes are missed). The
existing model table's P25 rows now let the signal's frames reach the native receiver (`fx_frames`): without frames a
P25 carrier is a foreign one, which this rule hides by design.

What it does not do: correct the strength itself. Unknown: whether the DM-32 reads -72 dBm everywhere or only on these
frequencies, and what DMR mode reads (a DMR stop counts the strength too: a high reading there makes every DMR stop
probe 700 ms). The single-slot cooldown is left as it is (the learned level covers its case).

### 5.1 A live DMR channel never caught in a mixed zone (second and third radio runs)

Zone of three: ch 1 DMR, ch 2 P25, ch 3 FM; nothing on the air, then the owner's other radio on ch 1 (DMR) and ch 2
(P25). P25 on ch 2 was caught with audio; DMR on ch 1 was **never** caught, fast or classic, although the radio plays
that call when it sits on ch 1 without scanning.

| Line | fast | classic |
|---|---|---|
| `scanst.cost` GAP_MAX, STEP_MAX, SWITCHES, BANDS | 169, 169, 339, 339 | 168, 0, 72, 71 |
| `scanst.quiet` DMR stops, ms | 170 stops, 27,742 ms (163 ms each) | 36 stops, 5,813 ms (161 ms each) |

Every step that switches the receiver between FM and DMR took 167-169 ms of the radio task (the tick's step in fast,
the poll's in classic: GAP_MAX). A DMR stop lasted 161-163 ms from its step's start, i.e. it had **no listening time
after the switch**: `scan_tuned` was given the step's start, the classic dwell (150 ms) was over when the step returned,
and the first poll moved on unless that one reading heard the DMR signal; after a fresh DMR start the modem cannot sync
for 390-600 ms (gotchas 17.2), so only the strength could, on one reading, half of which fall between direct-mode
bursts. In fast the stop's quiet span (60 ms) ended it even sooner. Not a regression: v1.0.1 timed it the same way.

Fix (`fsc_now`, both algorithms): after a step of `FS_STEP_SLOW_MS` (20 ms) or more, the stop's `tuned_ms` is the end of
the retune, so dwell, probe and hold count from there (a quick step keeps 1.0.2's timing). A second rule (the fast rule
never ending a stop that has just switched into DMR, `FS_DMR_FRESH`) was tried and is built off: the dwell fix alone
catches every over on the model in fast too, and the rule cost about 70 ms a DMR stop and failed the model table's
"fast catches at least what classic does" check by one over. Host model (`test_zonescan_dmr_switch`: this zone, a 168 ms mode
switch, DMR -60 dBm in overs, only the strength shows it): 1.0.2 fast **0 of 18** overs noticed (as on the radio),
classic 18 of 18 (the model's strength reading is instant; on the radio classic missed too, so the radio's DMR strength
right after the switch probably lags); now 18 of 18 both. Unknown: what the 168 ms are (the board's `set_mode` writes
look cheap; not found by reading); the new `scanst.step` line names the longest step's modes. If the DMR strength does
not rise at all within the dwell on the radio, a fresh DMR stop would need about 600 ms to sync: not done (it would slow
every mixed zone), measure first (SS-6).

To measure on the radio (read only, the owner's approval for each serial step; for 1.0.4 see section 6): the same zone
and 60 s on this build,
fast then classic: `scanst.cost` P25_LEARNED about 5, P25_MASKED climbing, PASS_MS / PASS_STOPS about 55 ms (fast) or 150 ms
(classic) after the first pass; native `takes` stops growing after the first pass. Then a DMR-only zone (if any) for 60 s:
`scanst.fb` REASON `strength` with an RSSI far above -100 dBm there means DMR has the same problem. And the Home meter's

## 6. "Starts slow, then speeds up" on 1.0.3 (branch `scanseed`, v1.0.4 candidate)

The owner's radio on 1.0.3 (2026-11-25), the P25 zone: the scan **starts slow, then speeds up**. That is section 5's
learning cost, as predicted there: each P25 channel needs two no-frame takes (about 1.9 s each: 1.2 s take + the 0.7 s
settle the next stop waits out) before its level is in force, so the first two passes are slow (about 19 s for five
channels); the same again every two minutes (a level expired 120 s after its last take, and once in force no take
refreshes it) and at every scan start (the table was cleared). **Host-tested only.**

Ideas weighed:

| Idea | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Seed the level from a quick strength average at the first stop, no take | rejected: without a take nothing tells the false strength from a real P25 carrier the noise reading is deaf to (gotchas 17.3); a call on the air when the scan starts would be hidden |
| Seed every P25 channel of the zone from the first one learned | rejected: the radio reads -62 .. -72 dBm on different channels, and a steady foreign carrier (an analog repeater) on one channel would hide the others |
| Learn from the first no-frame take | **taken, with a guard**: the reading of the first poll after the give back (the FM path again, about 1.2 s after the take began) must still show the strength, within 6 dB of the stop's strongest, with the noise reading quiet. The false strength stays; the call's tail that forced "confirm twice" (`test_zscan.inc`) has gone by then (no frame in 1.2 s means the carrier ended or is not ours), and a carrier the noise reading follows is not learned at once. Otherwise the take is only noted and a second one confirms, as in 1.0.3 |
| Keep the levels across scan restarts | **taken**: kept for the next scan of the same zone (keyed by zone position), cleared on another zone; the expiry and both drop rules still apply |
| Longer mask, refreshed while consistent | **taken** as a refresh: a masked reading not more than 6 dB over the learned level gives the level another 120 s. A reading 6-10 dB over (masked, but maybe a weak signal) does not, so such a level still expires |

What still protects a real signal (unchanged from 1.0.3): a reading 10 dB over the level is a carrier; the noise reading
counts as before; a reading with no strength (between a real station's overs) drops the level; a take that hears frames
drops it. New risk, small: a reply that keys up within ~1.2 s of a call's end, exactly when a tail take is given back,
would be learned at once and hidden until the next quiet visit of that channel (one round, 0.3-1 s in fast).

Verifier's review (2026-11-25), fixed on the branch: on the owner's channels the false strength stays over the line, so
"a reading with no strength" never comes there and could not drop a wrong level; and the refresh (any masked reading up
to 6 dB over the level) let the false strength itself, being under a wrongly high level, keep that level alive for good
(a level learned on a call's tail or on a foreign no-frame carrier, then calls at that strength never taken again; 1.0.3
lost such a level after 120 s). Now a reading more than 6 dB **under** the level drops it (it is not this channel's
steady strength), so the refresh only holds a level the readings sit within 6 dB of; and a codeplug change since the
last scan (a zone edited: other channels at these positions) clears the kept table. Model row
`test_zonescan_p25_seed_gone`: a no-frame carrier 25 dB over the false strength on the air as the scan starts, for
30 s, then 3 s P25 calls with frames at its strength every 10 s (16 calls), fast and classic the same: 1.0.3 noticed 5
(after the level's 120 s), the branch before the fix 0, now 16. The speed rows above are unchanged by the fix. Also found: the "first poll after the give
back" reads the receiver before the FM path is tuned again (`restore_channel` asks RREQ_RETUNE, tuned 400 ms later;
the scan steps on at that first poll): whether that strength and noise reading are fresh is the radio question below.
Left as it is: a level wrongly high by less than 6 dB (a weak call at the learning take) is still refreshed by the false
strength, so a call reading 10-16 dB over the false strength there stays masked (1.0.3: up to 120 s).

Code: `radio_scanfast.inc` (`fsc_p25_given_back` takes the whole status, `fsc_p25_masked` refreshes, `fsc_begin` keeps
the table; a level due later than 120 s from now is dropped as from another clock: the host tests restart theirs),
`scanfast.h` `FS_P25_SEED` (0 builds 1.0.3), one hook argument in `tools/plus_fastscan.py`. `native_lab.c` untouched.
